Four synthesized parts recreate detected notes. The original recording is never used in graph exports.

Any word = a hit · [dum ka tss] = fast group · *3 = repeat · :2 = hold two beats · rest or . = pause · CAPS = emphasis.

Try kick snare hat, do re mi, or your own syllables. Recognized syllables change pitch; other words use a neutral hit. This follows rhythm rules, not natural-language instructions.

What can I type?

Use x, pi, + − * / ^ and parentheses. Functions: sin, cos, tan, abs, exp, log, sqrt, floor, ceil, round, min, max, pow, mod and tri(x, center, width).

-2.8 + 4/(1+75*mod(x,1)^2)

Use * for multiplication: 2*sin(x). Non-finite values and values outside the visible y range create silent gaps. Drawn graphs use piece(x,x₀,y₀,x₁,y₁,…) for straight-line interpolation and join(…) for separate strokes.

Draw left to right. Lift your mouse or finger to leave a gap. One height per x; loops are converted to a function.
